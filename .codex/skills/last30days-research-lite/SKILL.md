# Last 30 Days Research Lite

This is a lightweight research workflow inspired by Last30Days.

## Workflow

1. Browse current sources. Do not rely on memory for recent claims.
2. Prefer primary sources, official docs, changelogs, GitHub repos, app listings, or reputable discussions.
3. Capture source URLs and publication or update dates when visible.
4. Separate facts from inference.
5. End with practical implications for Bahasa Buddy.

## Output Shape

- What changed recently.
- What matters for this app.
- Useful examples or patterns.
- Risks or caveats.
- Source links.

## Research Boundaries

- Do not scrape personal data.
- Do not summarize large copyrighted pages verbatim.
- For package or security recommendations, prefer official advisories and changelogs.
