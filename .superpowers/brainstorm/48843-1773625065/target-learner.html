Who is Bahasa Buddy for?

Understanding the target learner will shape the entire curriculum structure — pace, vocabulary focus, cultural depth, and skill priorities.

A

Tourist / Short-term Visitor

Traveling to Indonesia for weeks. Needs survival phrases: ordering food, directions, bargaining, emergencies. Speed over depth. ~200 key phrases.

B

Expat / Long-term Resident

Living or planning to live in Indonesia. Needs conversational fluency: daily life, workplace, social situations, reading signs/documents. Depth + breadth.

C

Heritage / Cultural Learner

Indonesian heritage or deep cultural interest. Wants to understand family conversations, media, literature. Emphasis on cultural context and formality levels.

D

General Language Learner (Duolingo-style)

Wants a structured A1→B2 progression like Duolingo or Babbel. Covers all skills systematically: reading, writing, listening, speaking. Gamified, self-paced.
